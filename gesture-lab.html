A reusable workshop for this hand and the next one

This page turns the tuning clinic into a repeatable workflow. It records normalized MediaPipe landmarks, derives conservative per-user thresholds, stores the profile in this browser, exports datasets, and replays old recordings against new rules.

No images are uploaded. Camera frames and landmarks remain in this browser unless you explicitly download a dataset.

1. CaptureCorrect poses and confusing impostors.

Implemented foundations

        
    
          
  • Stable IDs match hands by handedness, wrist position, and motion.
    • 
          
  • One Euro filtering smooths still hands without slowing fast motion.
    • 
          
  • Quality scoring rejects impossible geometry and landmark jumps.
    • 
          
  • Dropout grace preserves grabs through brief camera blur.
    • 
          
  • Time-based pinch and claw gates behave consistently across FPS.
    • 
          
  • Per-user profiles calibrate strong, separated feature "canyons."
    • 
          
  • Datasets store landmarks and feature vectors, not camera images.
    • 
          
  • Replay reports per-pose matches and false activations.

Future improvement roadmap

      

        
More usersCollect consented datasets across hands, cameras, lighting, skin tones, handedness, and mobility.

        
Learned classifierTrain on normalized landmark windows, then compare it with the rule engine in shadow mode.

        
MetricsTrack misses, false activations/hour, latency, hand swaps, and pointer jitter.

        
Sequence modelsUse short temporal windows for movement gestures rather than classifying isolated poses.

        
Accessible alternativesOffer configurable substitutes for clap and claw instead of forcing one anatomy or motion range.

        
Camera adaptationMeasure FPS, blur, exposure, and framing; offer actionable setup guidance before changing thresholds.
